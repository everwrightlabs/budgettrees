When to call

What counts as an emergency.

  • A tree or big limb on the house, a car, a fence or the driveway.
  • A trunk that’s cracked or split, or a tree suddenly leaning toward the house.
  • A broken limb hanging in the tree over a walkway, driveway or street.
  • A fallen tree blocking your driveway or the road.
  • Roots pulled up after a storm, with the tree still standing.

Wind in the forecast and a tree you’re worried about? Call before the storm. A safety trim costs a lot less than an emergency.

Good to know here

Storms, wind and power lines.

  • Power lines are Edison’s

    Never touch a tree that’s touching a wire. Southern California Edison clears trees from its own lines at no cost. For a downed line, call 911, then SCE at 1-800-611-1911.

    Report it to SCE

  • Who pays for the damage

    According to the Insurance Information Institute, your own homeowner’s policy covers damage to your house no matter whose tree it was. If the neighbor’s tree was clearly dead or diseased, your insurer may collect from theirs.

    Insurance Information Institute

  • Santa Ana season

    The first big wind of fall finds the weak limbs, and winter storms finish the job. A safety trim before the season costs a lot less than an emergency call.

Questions

Emergency questions.

Anything else? Call (818) 968-6997.

What counts as an emergency?

A tree or big limb on a house, car, fence or driveway, or one that’s cracked and hanging. Call (818) 968-6997 any time, day or night. If it’s touching a power line, stay well back and call 911 first.

A tree fell on a power line. Who do I call?

Call 911 first, then Southern California Edison at 1-800-611-1911. Stay far away, keep everyone back and don’t touch the tree. Once Edison has made the line safe, we can clear the tree.

My neighbor’s tree fell on my house. Who pays?

Usually your own homeowner’s insurance, no matter whose tree it was. If the tree was clearly dead or diseased and the neighbor ignored it, your insurer may go after theirs. Take photos before anything is moved.

How fast can you get here?

How much does emergency tree service cost?

Usually more than a scheduled job, because the tree is resting on something and has to come off carefully, often at night. You’ll know the price before we start cutting.
