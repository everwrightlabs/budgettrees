Questions

Good to know.

Anything else? Call (818) 968-6997.

Do you charge for estimates?

No. Estimates are free. Kelvin can usually price a job on the spot, or from a couple of photos you text us.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. California contractor license #684741, bonded and insured. We’re happy to send proof before we start.

Do I need a permit to trim or remove my tree?

For most trees on private property, no. Native oaks are the big exception: every city here protects them, and removing one, or cutting more than small branches, needs a permit first. Agoura Hills and Ventura County (Oak Park) protect sycamores too. Tell us what kind of tree it is and we’ll tell you what applies before any work starts.

Do you haul everything away?

Yes. Branches get chipped, logs get hauled, and we clean up before we leave. If you’d like to keep the wood or the mulch, just say so.
