When to call

Why grind it out?

  • It’s a trip hazard, and a mower-breaker.
  • It keeps sending up new shoots.
  • It’s rotting, and bringing ants and termites with it.
  • You want to plant, lay sod or build where the tree was.
  • It’s an old stump from a removal years ago that’s still in the way.

Good to know

Before and after the grinder.

  • Call 811 first

    California’s dig law covers any work that moves soil with equipment, and Edison’s advice is to call 811 before grinding a stump. It’s free, and the utilities mark their lines within two working days.

    DigAlert (811)

  • Let it settle

    Grindings left in the hole break down and sink over the next few months. For sod or pavers, have the chips hauled away and the hole filled with soil instead.

  • Keep chips off the house

    Wood chips make good mulch, just not right against the house. Fire experts want the first 5 feet around a home kept free of wood and mulch, and California’s proposed “Zone 0” rules would make that a requirement.

Questions

Stump questions.

Anything else? Call (818) 968-6997.

How much does stump grinding cost?

Mostly it depends on how wide the stump is and how many there are. Getting the grinder in matters too: a narrow gate, steps or a steep slope take longer. Text a photo with something for scale and Kelvin will send you a price.

Is it cheaper to grind a stump or dig it out?

Grinding, almost always. Digging a stump out means excavating the whole root ball, which takes longer and tears up the yard. It’s usually only worth it if something is being built right on that spot.

Will the tree grow back after the stump is ground?

Most won’t. A few, like eucalyptus and some poplars and willows, can send up shoots from the roots for a while. Cut them off as they come up and the roots eventually give up.

Do you need to call 811 before grinding a stump?

Yes. California’s dig law covers work that moves soil with equipment, and Edison tells people to call 811 before grinding stumps. It’s free, and it keeps the grinder away from gas, power and water lines.

Can I plant a new tree in the same spot?

Yes, once the grindings are cleared out and replaced with good soil. Fresh wood chips mixed into the soil can starve new plants of nitrogen for a while.
